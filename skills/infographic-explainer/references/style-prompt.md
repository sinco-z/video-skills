# Reusable visual brief

Single-canvas hand-drawn infographic explainer. White or subtly warm paper background, dark Chinese handwritten-looking text, slightly irregular rounded boxes, simple directional arrows. Pale yellow for selected cards and restrained red for the title and temporary underlining. Strong hierarchy, generous whitespace, readable at the planned zoom. No slide changes, decorative particles or generated image-to-video distortion.

Narration leads a gentle camera tour: whole board → relevant region → next semantic region → whole board. Hold while speaking. Draw one red underline with a following white cursor. Let it briefly remain, fade it away, then introduce the next mark. Keep subtitles fixed below the camera viewport.
