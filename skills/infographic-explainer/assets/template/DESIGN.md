# Visual direction

Reference: user supplied whiteboard infographic screenshots. A single continuous board, with restrained camera pans and zooms; no scene cards or decorative transitions.

## Colors
- Paper #fffef9
- Ink #252421
- Secondary ink #64615a
- Marker red #d92d20
- Pale yellow #ffed9e
- Gold ink #8c6c12

## Typography
Kaiti SC for the board, PingFang SC for subtitles. Handwritten-looking outlines, wide spacing, readable when zoomed.

## Motion
26 seconds, 30 fps, 1440 × 1080. Establish the full board, focus on the proposition, move to the income target, follow the service chain, and return to the whole board. Draw red underlines with a simulated cursor. Keep annotations in board coordinates. Subtitles remain fixed to the screen.

## What NOT to do
- No generative image-to-video effects.
- No flashing or continuous zooming.
- No cursor jitter.
- No asserted earnings promises in narration.
- Do not copy player controls from reference screenshots.
